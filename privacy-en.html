Luluno Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 23, 2026

Luluno (the “App”) is committed to providing a safe and enjoyable environment where children can enjoy drawing and coloring.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using the App.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as names, email addresses, home addresses, dates of birth, or location information.

No account registration or login is required to use the App.

The App does not use third-party advertising or analytics services and does not collect advertising identifiers for advertising purposes.

2. Drawings and Coloring Data

Drawings, coloring pages, and other content created using the App are processed and stored locally on the user’s device.

The App does not upload or store users’ artwork on servers operated by Luluno.

3. Saving to the Photo Library

Users may choose to save artwork created with the App to the device’s photo library.

Photo library functionality is used only when the user chooses to save their artwork.

4. Data When the App Is Deleted

If the App is deleted (uninstalled) from the device, artwork and other data stored within the App may also be deleted and may not be recoverable.

Artwork that has already been saved to the device’s photo library will not be deleted from the photo library when the App is uninstalled.

Users, parents, or guardians are encouraged to save important artwork to the photo library or another appropriate location before uninstalling the App or replacing the device.

5. Sharing Information with Third Parties

Because the App does not collect users’ personal information, it does not sell or provide collected personal information to third parties.

6. Advertising and Tracking

The App does not display third-party advertising.

The App does not track users across apps or websites owned by other companies for advertising or other tracking purposes.

7. Analytics

The App does not use third-party analytics services.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is designed for use by children.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children, including names, email addresses, home addresses, or location information.

The App does not provide account creation, social media posting, or communication between users.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to review the privacy settings of the device used by their child.

9. External Links

The App may provide links to external pages such as this Privacy Policy, the Terms of Use, or support information.

Where necessary, access to external content is provided through parental controls intended to prevent children from accessing external content without a parent or guardian.

10. Security

Luluno is committed to designing and operating the App with respect for users’ privacy and security.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when features are added or changed, or when applicable laws or services change.

If significant changes are made, notice may be provided within the App or through another appropriate method.
